PART A - is foreclosure status observable separately from field 4?
=========================================================================================================================
sources : docs/general_user_guide_july_2026.pdf
          docs/file_layout_july_2026.xlsx

A.1  FINDING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    No foreclosure-status field exists in the monthly performance file. Foreclosure progression is not observable as
    distinct from the delinquency counter at field 4 (index 3). Confirmed against the General User Guide and File Layout,
    July 2026, in docs/.

    Field number: none. Index: none. Enumeration: none. There is nothing to report
    on population during the delinquency period, because no such field exists.

A.2  FIELDS THAT TOUCH FORECLOSURE OR WORKOUT, AND WHY NONE OF THEM IS THAT FIELD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    field  4 (index  3)  Current Loan Delinquency Status
        carries 'RA' = REO Acquisition. RA is the completion of foreclosure (title taken), not a process stage, and it
        is this same field rather than a distinct one.

    field  7 (index  6)  Underwriting Defect and Major Servicing Defect Settlement Date
        a repurchase / make-whole settlement date. Unrelated to foreclosure process, and populated at settlement only.

    field  9 (index  8)  Zero Balance Code
        02 / 03 / 09 identify a foreclosure-related TERMINATION after the fact. Blank until the terminating event, so
        it carries no in-process signal.

    field 10 (index  9)  Zero Balance Effective Date
        the period of the terminating event. Terminal only.

    field 25 (index 24)  Payment Deferral Flag
        a workout instrument, and the opposite of foreclosure.

    field 29 (index 28)  Delinquency Due to Disaster
        hardship / forbearance flag. Guide: 'Only populated for January 2014 and following periods.'

    field 30 (index 29)  Borrower Assistance Plan
        F = Forbearance, R = Repayment, T = Trial Period. The closest thing in the file to a during-delinquency process
        marker, but it is a RETENTION workout, not foreclosure, and the Guide states 'Only populated for January 2014
        and following periods' - i.e. null across the entire 2007-2011 window these vintages exist to observe.

A.3  CONSEQUENCE FOR PART B
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Foreclosure timing is not separately observable during the delinquency period. Between entry into DQ90+ and the
    terminal event, field 4 is the only state signal the file carries, so 'months in DQ90+' is simultaneously arrears
    depth and elapsed foreclosure clock, and the two cannot be separated in this data. The process is observed only at
    its endpoint: 'RA' in field 4 at REO acquisition, or a zero-balance code at termination. A run-length of k months in
    DQ90+ therefore cannot be read as 'k months since foreclosure referral'.

A.4  FIELD 4 (INDEX 3) - THE GUIDE'S EXACT WORDS, INCLUDING CAP BEHAVIOUR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Current Loan Delinquency Status
    
      Description
        "A value corresponding to the number of days the borrower is delinquent,
         based on the Due Date of Last Paid Installment ("DDLPI") reported by
         servicers to Freddie Mac.
    
         If a loan is acquired through REO, then the Current Loan Delinquency
         Status will reflect "RA" for REO Acquisition (instead of the value
         corresponding to the number of days the borrower is delinquent).
    
         This value for any given month will be capped at 99.
    
         Note: For periods on or before September 2025, 00 could mean either
         Current or Cycle before first payment date."
    
      Enumerations
        "00 = Current, or less than 30 days delinquent
         01 = 30-59 days delinquent
         02 = 60 - 89 days delinquent
         03 = 90 - 119 days delinquent etc.
         RA = REO Acquisition
    
         XX = Not Available"

    The cap sentence, quoted alone, since the DQ90+ definition in Part B rests on it:

        "This value for any given month will be capped at 99."

    Read with the enumeration ('03 = 90 - 119 days delinquent etc.'), 99 is the top rung of the same ladder - 99 or more
    months delinquent - and not a missing-value sentinel; the Guide supplies 'XX = Not Available' for that purpose. The
    Guide says nothing special about 98 at all, which is itself the point: 98 is simply the ordinary rung below the cap.
    Both therefore fold into DQ90+ with every other status >= 3, matching decision D12 and diagnostic 06.

    The cap has moved historically: the release notes record 'Remove the Delinquency Status cap of D180', so the 99 cap
    quoted above is the current regime, which is the one these files are published under.

B.3  FOOTNOTES - counts behind the construction
=========================================================================================================================
(a) Runs broken by a reporting gap
      1,354 DQ90+ loan-months open a new run because the immediately preceding month was
      never reported, although the previous observed row for that loan was itself
      DQ90+. The counter restarts at 1 at each of these, as briefed. Runs broken by an
      OBSERVED non-DQ90+ month are ordinary restarts and are not counted here.
      Share of all DQ90+ loan-months: 0.0209%

(b) Outcomes classified across a reporting gap
      1,486 DQ90+ loan-months have their next OBSERVED row more than one month later, so
      their 'state next month' is in fact 'state at next observation'. Share of all
      DQ90+ loan-months: 0.0230%. By outcome:
        Cure                 87
        Stay              1,058
        Default             332
        Prepaid               3
        Censored              6

(c) Terminal DQ90+ rows
      117,581 DQ90+ loan-months are themselves the loan's zero-balance row, and are
      classified from their own code rather than as censored-at-cutoff. Where the row
      before one of these is also DQ90+, the same exit is attributed at two adjacent
      run-lengths. This count is the upper bound on that overlap.

(d) DQ90+ loan-months at or after a first RA
      7. RA is not a numeric status, so post-RA months cannot be DQ90+; a non-zero
      value here would mean a loan returned to a numeric status after REO acquisition
      (D8 records 2 such loans in 118,026 across the panel).

(e) Duplicate (loan, period) rows
      0, among rows belonging to DQ90+ loans. A repeated period neither continues nor
      gap-breaks a run.

(f) Zero-balance values outside the documented enumeration
      0 rows, among rows belonging to DQ90+ loans. Documented enumeration is
      01 / 02 / 03 / 09 / 15 / 16 / 96, plus blank while the loan is still active.

(g) Unclassified DQ90+ loan-months
      0. A loan-month lands here only if its successor row carries no zero-balance
      code and a field 4 that is neither numeric nor RA ('XX = Not Available', or
      blank).
      Zero, so the five outcome rates sum to 100% in every bucket.
