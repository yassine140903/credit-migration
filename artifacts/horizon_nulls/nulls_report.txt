MODEL DEFINITIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
persistence  identity matrix chained by src.horizon.chain /
             homogeneous_step - the baseline's own path. Every loan keeps
             its state at the cut with probability 1.0 at every horizon.
marginal     the empirical distribution of next_state across ALL fit rows
             (role == fit), applied to every loan regardless of its state
             at the cut, identical at every horizon; not chained.
baseline     the pooled matrix from src.baseline.load_baseline(), chained.
ORACLE (not achievable)
             NOT A MODEL - a diagnostic floor built from TEST-PERIOD
             OUTCOMES; it could not be built at forecast time. For each
             horizon AND each cohort separately: the realised state
             distribution across that cohort, given identically to every
             loan in it. Not chained; ignores the state at the cut.
             Unlike the marginal null (ONE fit-window distribution for
             every horizon and cohort), it DIFFERS per horizon and per
             cohort.
